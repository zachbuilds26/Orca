How it works

From sentence to settlement.

One narrow flow: capture the rule, confirm the wallet, and execute on testnet when the market hits.

  1. 01

    Type the rule

    A plain sentence becomes a structured buy intent with amount, trigger, and expiry.

  2. 02

    Confirm the wallet

    Telegram or a browser handoff verifies one EVM address, then Orca uses it for every live intent.

  3. 03

    Watch execution

    Orca checks the live OKB price and sends a sponsored X Layer testnet transfer when the trigger hits.
