Connecting to Orca

You will sign a one-time ownership message. It is not a transaction, does not share your private key, and does not grant Orca permission to spend from your wallet.

What connection means

  • Your verified address becomes Orca’s testnet transfer recipient.
  • Orca still uses its own funded testnet execution wallet.
  • You can disconnect at any time; live intents tied to this wallet are paused.
